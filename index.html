🎮 How the XP System Works

GrowthOS uses behavioral psychology to build permanent habits via positive reinforcement.

How to earn XP:

  • Daily Tasks: Low priority (+15 XP), Medium (+30 XP), High (+50 XP).
  • Deep Focus: Finish a 25-minute sprint (+50 XP). Finish a 50+ min sprint (+100 XP).
  • Overachieving: Pushing a target goal past 100% unlocks secret status tiers.

Leveling Formula:

Levels curve exponentially. The formula is: Level = floor(sqrt(XP / 50)) + 1. This means early levels are fast, but elite levels require compounding consistency.

The Penalty:

If you uncheck a completed habit, you instantly lose the XP. If your total tasks completed drops to zero for the day, your daily streak is mathematically revoked!
